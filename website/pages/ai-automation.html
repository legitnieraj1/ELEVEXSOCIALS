AI Automation FAQs

Common questions about implementing AI automation

How long does it take to set up AI automation for my business?

Setup time varies based on complexity. Simple chatbots can be deployed in 1-2 weeks, while comprehensive automation systems with multiple integrations typically take 3-6 weeks. We work aggressively to show you results quickly, often with initial chatbots live within 10 days.

Do I need technical knowledge to use these automation systems?

No technical knowledge required. We handle all setup, integration, and maintenance. We provide simple dashboards and training for your team to monitor performance and adjust settings. You interact with simple interfaces, not code.

Will AI automation replace my customer support team?

AI automation doesn't replace your team—it enhances them. Chatbots handle routine inquiries and qualify leads, freeing your team to focus on complex customer issues and relationship building. Many businesses reallocate support staff to higher-value work rather than laying them off.

How much does AI automation cost for my business?

Pricing depends on the scope and complexity of automation. Basic chatbot setups start at affordable monthly subscriptions, while comprehensive systems may require initial setup fees plus monthly maintenance. Most clients see ROI within 2-4 months through time savings and revenue increases.

What happens if something breaks or needs modification?

We provide ongoing support and maintenance as part of our service. We monitor your automations, fix issues quickly, and make adjustments based on performance data. You have direct access to our team for questions and improvements.
