Our Branding Process

A proven methodology for creating exceptional brands

01

We conduct in-depth interviews and workshops to understand your business, values, vision, and target audience. This foundation is critical for creating an authentic brand.

02

Strategy Development

Based on discovery insights, we develop your brand strategy including positioning statement, brand values, personality, and messaging framework that guides all creative decisions.

Branding FAQs

Common questions about our branding services

How long does a complete branding project take?

A complete branding project typically takes 4-8 weeks depending on the scope and complexity. Our branding process includes discovery, strategy, concept development, refinement, and brand guidelines delivery. We keep you involved throughout with regular feedback sessions.

What's included in your branding package?

Our comprehensive branding package includes brand strategy, logo design (multiple concepts), visual identity system, brand guidelines, stationery designs, and a social media branding kit. We also provide all design files in various formats for implementation across channels.

Do you offer logo redesign for existing businesses?

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in logo redesigns that maintain brand equity while refreshing the visual identity. Whether you need a subtle modernization or a complete rebrand, we ensure the new logo honors your brand heritage while appealing to current and future customers.

Can I get revisions on the logo design?

Yes, revisions are included throughout our design process. We typically provide 2-3 rounds of revisions to logo concepts and additional refinement rounds based on your feedback. We continue refining until you're completely satisfied with the final design.

What happens after the branding project is complete?

We deliver comprehensive brand guidelines that teach your team how to use the brand correctly. We can also help with implementation across websites, marketing materials, and social media. Our ongoing support ensures brand consistency as your business evolves.
