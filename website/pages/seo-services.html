SEO Questions Answered

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is not instant. Most businesses see initial results in 2-3 months, with significant improvements by 6 months. Competitive keywords may take 6-12 months. We focus on sustainable growth, not quick fixes.

What's the difference between SEO and SEM?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is organic—you earn rankings through optimization. SEM (Search Engine Marketing) includes paid ads. We recommend both for maximum visibility. SEO is cost-effective long-term.

Can you guarantee #1 ranking?

No legitimate agency guarantees rankings. Google's algorithm is complex and ever-changing. We guarantee honest work, proven strategies, and measurable improvements. We aim for page 1 results in your target keywords.

Do you do SEO for e-commerce sites?

Yes! E-commerce SEO is a specialty. We optimize product pages, category structures, implement rich snippets, and improve conversion rates. E-commerce sites have unique SEO challenges we handle expertly.

What's included in your SEO package?

Our packages include keyword research, site audits, on-page optimization, technical SEO fixes, content creation, local SEO, link building, and monthly reporting. Exact inclusions vary by package.
