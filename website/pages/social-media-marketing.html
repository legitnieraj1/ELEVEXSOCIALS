Social Media Questions Answered

How much should I budget for social media marketing?

It depends on your goals and industry. Small businesses typically start with 500-2000/month. Our team will recommend a budget after understanding your goals and competition.

Which platforms should my business be on?

Not every business needs every platform. We recommend platforms based on where your audience is and where you can create consistent, valuable content. Quality over quantity.

How often should I post on social media?

Frequency depends on the platform and your audience. Instagram typically 4-5x/week, LinkedIn 3-4x/week, Facebook 1-2x/day. We create a schedule based on your strategy.

Can social media marketing generate leads and sales?

Absolutely. Social media drives leads, sales, and customers. We optimize for conversions through strategic ads, compelling landing pages, and community engagement.

What kind of content performs best on social media?

It varies by platform and audience, but video, behind-the-scenes content, user testimonials, and interactive polls typically perform well. We test and optimize based on your audience data.
